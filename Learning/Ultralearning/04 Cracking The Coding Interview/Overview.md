### Phase 1 - Reading through CTCI
#### Step One - Retrieval Practice
*SKIP FOR FIRST SESSION*
1. Look at your tracker, see the oldest topic (longest time since last practice). 
2. Pick a problem from that set and attempt close book on paper.
3. Review answer, mark results as wrong, partial answer or fully correct. 
4. Note where you went wrong and note study focus in future (time to hit your additional resources or create new flash cards)
#### Step Two - New Material
1. Pick a chapter you haven't done yet.
2. Prime through the material (skim read headers + copy problem set into notes spend + 10 mins on the first problem).
3. Go back and read through the chapter in full. Only notes allowed are words / concepts you don't understand well enough to explain out loud. 
4. Close book and hide your notes once you are done.
5. Do 10 minutes of free recall on what you remember about what you learned.
6. Compare with notes with chapter.
7. Create flash cards for important concepts stuff you don't already know!
8. Work on a problem.
9. If you get stuck, think deeply about why and and what you could retain so you were not stuck in the future
10. Record results in tracker (Correct, partially correct, not even close).
11. If your stuck or confused, try
	1. Thinking of a sticky metaphor.
	2. Think of how you could apply this to your projects.
#### Step Three - Reflection
- Spend 10 mins jotting down answers the the following reflections
	- What did I find hard, how would I find it easier next time?
	- How does this relate to what I already know?
	- How could I use this in my projects or personal life?
