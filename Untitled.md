# **What I’d recommend for you (more effective than a generic elimination diet)**

Instead of a broad “cut everything” approach, you’ll get much clearer answers with a **low-FODMAP elimination phase (2–4 weeks)** followed by structured reintroduction.

This is the gold-standard dietary approach for IBS.

## **Why this is better for you**

A typical elimination diet removes:

- dairy, gluten, eggs, nuts, etc.

But IBS triggers are more often:

- onion/garlic
- wheat (not gluten itself, but fructans)
- lactose (very relevant given ice cream suspicion)
- certain fruits
- legumes
- polyols (sweeteners, stone fruit)

So you can cut the _wrong things_ and still feel bad.

# **Phase 2: reintroduction (this is where answers come from)**

After 2–4 weeks symptom-stable:

You reintroduce **one FODMAP group at a time**, not individual foods randomly.

Example order:

1. Lactose (milk, ice cream — your key suspicion)
2. Fructans (wheat, garlic, onion)
3. Fructose (fruit like mango, honey)
4. Polyols (stone fruits, sweeteners)
5. GOS (beans/legumes)

This is where we’ll actually identify your triggers.
