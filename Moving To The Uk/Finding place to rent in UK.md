**Three separate corridors** serve your destination:

**1. Victoria line (Tube) — direct to Pimlico, no transfer**  
Running northeast from Brixton through central London to Walthamstow. Already covered: Brixton, Stockwell (nearby), Highbury & Islington area. The stations we identified north of Finsbury Park — Seven Sisters, Tottenham Hale, Blackhorse Road, Walthamstow — were covered.

**2. Southern Rail (National Rail) — direct to Victoria station, then ~10 min walk to Eccleston Square**  
This is the big gap in the previous tables. Southern runs two main affordable corridors:

- **Brighton Main Line via Clapham Junction:** Clapham Junction → Battersea Park → Victoria. Suburbs further out on this line include **Balham, Wandsworth Common, Streatham Hill, Streatham Common, Norbury, Thornton Heath** — all direct to Victoria.
- **Chatham Main Line via Herne Hill:** Herne Hill → Tulse Hill → Brixton (rail) → Victoria. Suburbs: **Herne Hill, Tulse Hill, West Dulwich, West Norwood, Gipsy Hill, Crystal Palace**.

**The standout additions at your £1,500 budget:**

**Streatham / Streatham Common / Norbury / Thornton Heath** are the best value in all of London for a direct Victoria commute. Studios from ~£1,000, 1-beds regularly under £1,400. Thornton Heath in particular is very affordable but still only ~30 minutes on a direct train.

**Crystal Palace and West Norwood / Gipsy Hill** are great mid-range options with a strong community feel, nice Victorian housing stock, and a direct service into Victoria in around 20–25 minutes.

**Tulse Hill and Herne Hill** are worth a look — Herne Hill in particular is gentrifying quickly and has a lovely village feel, but you'll need to move fast as it's becoming popular and prices are climbing past £1,500 for 1-beds.
