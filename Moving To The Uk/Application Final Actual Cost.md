You can upload your supporting documents online free of charge, before your appointment day.  
Please refer to the document checklist you received as part of your online application on GOV.UK, so you know which documents are needed, and follow the instructions on the Document Upload pages.  
Important information for uploading documents online:  
1) Please refer to the Document Checklist you received from GOV.UK as part of your application.  
2) We’d suggest uploading no more than five documents at once, and into one category at a time.  
3) We can’t accept encrypted, password-protected, or 'Read-only' files. Please upload a scan of the printed document if you can't remove these restrictions.  
4) Please ensure your document name is in English and does not include special characters.  
5) You can upload PDFs/JPG/JPEG/PNGs up to 5MB. If your file exceeds this limit, we recommend scanning at 150 - 200 DPI or uploading the file in separate parts to reduce the size.  
6) If you close your browser, you’ll lose your progress. If you can, upload all of your files in one session.
