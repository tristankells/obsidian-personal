#### Hack: Meerkat Movies 🏆 Best casual deal

Buy certain insurance, switch utilities, or apply for a financial product through Compare the Market and you get 2-for-1 cinema tickets on a Tuesday or Wednesday for a full year. The trick is to buy a one-night UK travel insurance policy — which can cost as little as £1 — to qualify for a year's membership. [MoneySavingExpert](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/deals/cinema-tips-and-tricks/)[MoneySavingExpert](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/deals/2for1-meerkat-movies-cinema-and-meerkat-meals-restaurant-trick/)

So if you go with a friend on a Tuesday/Wednesday: **Vue Piccadilly tickets become ~£5 each.** That's the cheapest per-movie option for casual viewing.

You can stack it with other deals, such as paying with discounted gift cards to save even more. [Be Clever With Your Cash](https://becleverwithyourcash.com/get-2-for-1-cinema-tickets-year/)

#### Amazon Prime deal

Amazon Prime members can get 2 standard Odeon tickets for £10 or 2 Luxe tickets for £15 on Monday–Thursday, once per calendar month. That's £5/ticket — same as Meerkat but only usable once a month. [Capital Matters](https://www.capitalmatters.co.uk/posts/odeon-cinema-deals)

#### Octopus Energy deal

Octopus Energy customers can get 2 standard Odeon tickets for £10 every week (Monday–Thursday) as a free Octopus Reward. If you end up switching energy providers when you land, this is worth knowing about — effectively £5/ticket weekly with no extra sign-up needed. [Capital Matters](https://www.capitalmatters.co.uk/posts/odeon-cinema-deals)

Note: You can't use a myLimitless membership ticket to also get a free second ticket with Meerkat Movies — they can't be stacked. [Be Clever With Your Cash](https://becleverwithyourcash.com/are-cinema-memberships-worth-the-money/)

### My Recommendation for You

Given you're in Pimlico with Curzon Victoria 10 minutes away and the West End chains 20–25 mins away:

**If you go 1–2x/month casually:** The **£1 Meerkat trick** is unbeatable. One cheap travel insurance policy → a year of 2-for-1 on Tue/Wed at Vue, Odeon, Cineworld. ~£5/ticket, total annual "cost" = £1.

**If you go 3–4x/month:** **Odeon myLimitless at £16.99/mo** starts paying off. Add the West End upgrade if you want the Haymarket Luxe recliners.

**If you love arthouse/indie + you'll use Curzon a lot:** The **Curzon Cult at £285/yr** gives you 7 tickets a week _and_ covers their home streaming service, which is a nice bonus.

**Wildcard:** If you switch to Octopus Energy anyway (very popular/cheap in the UK), the weekly 2-for-1 Odeon perk is essentially free.
