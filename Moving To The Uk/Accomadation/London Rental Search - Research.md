The three websites that dominate the London rental market are:

### 1. [Rightmove](https://www.rightmove.co.uk?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Best overall choice**

- Largest inventory of rental properties in London.
- Most estate agents list here.
- Excellent map search and commute-time filters.
- New listings appear throughout the day.

If you're serious about finding a place, Rightmove should be your primary site.

---

### 2. [Zoopla](https://www.zoopla.co.uk?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Best secondary search**

- Many of the same listings as Rightmove.
- Sometimes has listings that appear slightly earlier or aren't on Rightmove.
- Useful local market information and price history.

I recommend checking both Rightmove and Zoopla daily.

---

### 3. [OpenRent](https://www.openrent.co.uk?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Best for avoiding agency fees and dealing directly with landlords**

- Direct landlord listings.
- Often cheaper than agency-managed properties.
- Good for flats, studios, and house shares.

Competition can be fierce because tenants like dealing directly with landlords.

## A few London rental tips

- Good rentals often receive multiple viewing requests within **24–48 hours**.
- Have your documents ready (passport, visa if applicable, employment details, references).
- Set up instant email alerts on Rightmove and Zoopla.
- Don't rely solely on postcode averages; two streets in the same area can feel very different.
- If your budget allows, prioritize being within a 10–15 minute walk of a Tube or Overground station.

### Why?

**Battersea**

- Probably the best location on your list for working near Victoria.
- Easy bus, train, bike, or Tube connections.
- Huge amount of regeneration around Battersea Power Station.
- Lots of restaurants and parks.

**Clapham Junction**

- One of London's best-connected stations.
- Extremely easy commute to Victoria.
- Plenty of rental stock.

**Balham**

- Often considered a "sweet spot."
- Good nightlife and restaurants.
- Direct trains to Victoria.
- More affordable than nearby Clapham.

**Herne Hill**

- One of London's nicest villages.
- Adjacent to Brockwell Park.
- Strong community feel.
- Excellent balance of commute and lifestyle.

**Wandsworth Common**

- Green, attractive, safe-feeling.
- Similar commute benefits to Balham.

**Brixton**

- Best nightlife and food scene on the list.
- Victoria Line commute is excellent.
- More urban and energetic than Balham or Herne Hill.
