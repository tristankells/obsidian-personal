### A Few Practical Tips for Your Situation

Since you're relocating from NZ and will likely need 1–3 months while you find a permanent place:

- **Spotahome** is probably your best starting point — they do virtual tours and verified listings, which matters when you're booking from overseas before you arrive.
- **Airbnb monthly stays** are worth checking — hosts often give 20–40% discounts for 28+ day bookings, and you get flexibility with no formal tenancy.
- **SpareRoom** has a "short let" filter and is good if you're open to a room rather than a whole flat initially — much cheaper and lets you get a feel for different areas before committing.
- Avoid signing anything long-term until you've spent a few weeks in London and can visit neighbourhoods in person. The difference between reading about Tooting and actually living near the high street on a Saturday morning is significant.
