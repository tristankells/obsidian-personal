**Landlord's contents-only / gap policies**

ASB's Landlord's Contents policy is explicitly designed for a rental home already covered by a separate body corporate or strata policy — it covers loss of rent in certain situations and lets you add a rent-default option on top, without you paying for building cover you already have through the body corporate. Its Landlord's Contents cover includes legal liability, landlord's fixtures and fittings, loss of rent, and methamphetamine contamination — which lines up closely with the gaps we identified. [asb](https://asb.co.nz/home-and-contents-insurance/landlord-protection.html)[asb](https://asb.co.nz/home-and-contents-insurance/landlord-protection.html)

**Full landlord policies (if you want to compare against a contents-only option)**

Most mainstream NZ insurers sell a bundled landlord policy that includes building cover, so you'd be paying for some duplication, but they're worth pricing for comparison:

- **Initio** — landlord insurance including loss of rent up to 12 months or $20,000, deliberate damage up to $25,000, $30,000 of meth cleaning cover, and $20,000 of landlord contents cover as standard, underwritten by IAG New Zealand [initio](https://initio.co.nz/landlord-insurance)
- **Tower** (Landlords Plus), **AA Insurance**, **AMI**, **AMP** — all offer comparable landlord products; typical cover limits across these run to $20,000-25,000 for deliberate tenant damage, up to 8-12 months loss of rent for building damage, up to 8 weeks for tenant default, up to $30,000 for meth decontamination, and up to $20,000 for landlord contents [calculate](https://www.calculate.co.nz/landlord-insurance-calculator.php)

**What to specifically ask about when getting quotes**

1. Whether they'll write a **contents/gap-only policy** (like ASB's) given the building is already insured via the body corporate — this avoids paying twice for the same structure cover
2. **Rent default** cover as an add-on (not just "loss of rent due to damage") — this is the piece the body corporate endorsement genuinely doesn't touch
3. **Meth testing/decontamination** limits
4. **Personal landlord liability** limits (separate from the body corporate's liability cover)
